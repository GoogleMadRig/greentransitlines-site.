Our Fleet

Our fleet acquisition is a sophisticated, mixed-fleet approach designed to deploy the optimal trainset for each specific service mission, maximizing both capacity and operational flexibility.

greenbulletlines (Express Service)

Our premier express service will feature a distinct fleet of world-class, high-performance trainsets capable of speeds up to 220 mph, such as the Hitachi AT400 or ETR1000 Frecciarossa.

greenlinklines (Regional Connector)

For longer-distance regional services that will connect the network to new termini like Jacksonville and Tampa, we will procure a fleet of hybrid-bimodal trains, such as the Hitachi Blues. Their ability to operate seamlessly on both electrified and non-electrified track sections is essential for providing a one-seat ride on these new corridors.

greenlinklines (High-Capacity Commuter)

For the high-density, fully-electrified core commuter routes in South Florida and Central Florida, we will procure high-capacity, all-electric, double-deck trainsets like the Siemens Desiro HC and Hitachi Caravaggio.

About Us

Our Mission

To deliver a new era of American high-speed and commuter passenger rail by launching a clean, all-electric and biodiesel-electric and globally competitive rail system that offers a fast, reliable, and inclusive alternative to driving and short-haul flights — starting in Florida and expanding nationwide.

Our Vision

To become the first unified high-speed rail network in the United States that meets international benchmarks for safety, reliability, speed, sustainability, and service — while connecting cities, boosting regional economies, and setting a new standard for modern transportation infrastructure.
